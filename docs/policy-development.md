# Governance, Policies, and Operational Procedures

## Overview

As founder and CERT Manager of UBT-CERT, I drafted and implemented governance documents, security policies, and operational procedures supporting the team’s establishment and preparation for Trusted Introducer (TI) recognition and FIRST membership.

This work connected the CERT’s mission and organizational structure with practical requirements for information protection, incident handling, secure communication, collaboration, and staff development.

The inventory below summarizes the historical documents and topics covered. Titles have been standardized for readability while retaining their original meaning.

## My contribution

* Drafted and implemented information classification, protection, and dissemination policies.
* Defined security event and incident classification and prioritization practices.
* Established guidance for secure communication and the protection of information shared with other teams.
* Prepared organizational and operational documentation supporting international membership processes.
* Developed incident reporting, response, tracking, and acknowledgment procedures.
* Addressed physical security, network infrastructure, and professional development within the CERT’s operating framework.

## Connection to Membership Preparation

These documents formed part of the governance and operational preparation supporting UBT-CERT’s progression through Trusted Introducer recognition and FIRST membership.

The historical project report describes a ten-month preparation period, from December 2016 through September 2017, covering policy drafting, implementation, and work addressing 29 FIRST membership prerequisites.

This recovered inventory contains six TI-related entries and 22 FIRST-related entries. Several subjects appear in both groups. The inventory is therefore a record of documented work areas rather than a one-to-one reconstruction of the 29 prerequisites.

## Historical Context

The numbered references above are preserved from the recovered inventory. They are not presented as the numbering of current FIRST requirements.

References to ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27002, and ITIL describe the foundations used in the work; they do not constitute a claim of organizational certification.

This page summarizes historical work undertaken at UBT-CERT. It does not reproduce internal operational policies or describe the team’s current procedures.
