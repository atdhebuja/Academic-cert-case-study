# Rights, attribution and scope
This is Atdhe Buja's personal retrospective portfolio, not an official repository of UBT, UBT-CERT, FIRST or Trusted Introducer. Names identify the organizations relevant to the historical work; no endorsement is implied.

Original institutional documents, third-party training materials, certificates, logos and student work retain their respective ownership. Authorship or possession alone does not establish permission to republish every item. Prefer public source links and original summaries where redistribution is unclear.

No blanket software or content license is applied by this starter. Any later license should clearly state which original materials it covers and exclude third-party content.

Before adding historical documents, remove internal addressing, credentials, incident/victim details and unnecessary student or staff personal information. Preserve a private original and publish only a reviewed copy. Screenshots should be cropped to the relevant exhibit, excluding browser tabs and unrelated personal information.

New templates or recreated diagrams must carry their actual creation date and be labeled as retrospective or illustrative; they should not be presented as recovered original policies.
